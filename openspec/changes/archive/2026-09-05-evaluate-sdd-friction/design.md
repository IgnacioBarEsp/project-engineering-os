## Context and decision

The approved scope is evaluation and design, not installation. Use upstream archived assessments, resolved
scoped-rule debt and cross-platform CI failures as bounded engineering observations. None prove user pain,
productivity or the external benchmark complete. Publish a guide rather than seed an empty map.

## Model

Keep factual installed steps separate from opportunity records. Each optional record names the step,
signal, hypothesis/validated status, source, intervention (automatiza/reduce-friccion/no-ia/prohibido),
human decision, owner, review trigger and fallback. Product journeys are consumer-owned and may be
not applicable with a reason. Repeated FAIL is a candidate signal requiring diagnosis.

## Ownership and alternatives

Document installed process in managed, versioned reference material; observations belong in a consumer
document chosen by the team and are never overwritten. Defer a machine-readable schema and bootstrap
integration until actual consumer use establishes a stable contract. A missing map never becomes debt
just because N flows elapsed. Avoid an empty mandatory template and automatic pain inference.

## Validation and risk

Review every example against its local source and label constructed examples. Check published links,
neutrality, strict OpenSpec and normal repository validation. Self-review must not be represented as
independent review. Revert only guide, ADR and links if misleading; no state migration is necessary.
